Meet the artist

Hi, I'm Camille

I have been doing makeup for a decade — nine hundred-odd faces, roughly four hundred of them brides. What I have learned is that nobody wants to look "done." They want to look like themselves on their best-lit day.

So I work skin-first: proper prep, thin buildable layers, and colour matched in daylight rather than under a ring light. Everything in my kit is chosen for how it behaves after hour six, not how it swatches in the pan.

— Camille
